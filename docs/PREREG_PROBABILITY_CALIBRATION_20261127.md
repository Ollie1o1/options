# Preregistration — Study 4B: Walk-Forward Empirical Probability Calibration for `[6] DISCOVER`

**Registered:** 2026-11-27 (frozen before running `scripts/backtest_probability_calibration.py`).

## Why

Showing a single "% chance of success" in the UI without backtesting whether predicted probabilities match realized outcomes misleads traders (e.g., Black-Scholes `1 - |delta|` failed reliability in 4 of 6 strategies in `src/pop_calibration.py`). This study tests **walk-forward calibrated probabilities** across every active sleeve & DTE bucket, and enforces a strict **calibration gate (`ship_check`)**: only probabilities that match realized out-of-sample hit rates earn a `CALIBRATED` badge in Discover.

## Hard Honesty Guardrail (Immutable UI Contract)

* Every `%` shown in `[6] DISCOVER` MUST come from `configs/calibrated_evidence.json` generated by `scripts/backtest_probability_calibration.py`.
* Every `P(Win)` MUST be displayed together with **`95% CI`**, **`n`**, **`PF` / Expected Return**, and **`Avg Loss` (Tail Risk)** — never a standalone win rate.
* Any DTE bucket or strategy whose out-of-sample `x_entry <= 0` or `PF <= 1.0` MUST be labeled **`CLOSED / NEGATIVE EV`** even if its raw win rate is high (e.g., `85%+`).
